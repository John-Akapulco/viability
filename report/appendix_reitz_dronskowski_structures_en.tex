\noindent\textsuperscript{a}~includes a third-shell Sn-Sn contribution ($d=3.54$--$3.57$~\AA, $-1.336$~eV/formula unit) that the manuscript's own accounting (6 Zn-Sn bonds $+$ 1 Zn-Zn bond per formula unit, no Sn-Sn term) does not include. Excluding this term, ZnSn alone drops to $-16.908$~eV, within $0.2\%$ of the manuscript.

Replacing the default Sn reference ($\beta$-tin, a different polymorph)
with the $\alpha$-tin the manuscript implicitly prescribes, computed
under its exact methodology, drops the Sn discrepancy from 38.4\% to
0.4\% --- almost the entire Sn gap was therefore a polymorph error, not
a genuine DFT disagreement. ZnSn alone also drops to 0.2\% once the
Sn-Sn term absent from the manuscript's own accounting is isolated
(note a). Zn, however, remains an open residual: the 36.0\%
discrepancy persists even though our manuscript-methodology-relaxed
geometry reproduces Table~S1 to better than 0.15\%
(Table~\ref{tab:reitz-dronskowski-structures}) --- near-perfect
geometric agreement does not resolve the ICOHP gap for this element.
The most likely explanation, not yet verified, is a difference in
coordination-shell counting convention between our automated detection
(first Zn-Zn shell lumping the basal $2.608$~\AA\ and interlayer
$2.729$--$2.734$~\AA\ contacts, very close in distance) and the
manuscript's own convention --- worth investigating if this validation
is picked back up. The recomputed $\Delta$ICOHP for ZnSn$\to$Zn+Sn
(1.9$\times$ the manuscript) improves markedly over the old calculation
(2.9$\times$) but remains dominated by this unresolved Zn residual, not
by a global DFT-methodology problem (\S\ref{sec:reframing}'s 7-case
validation, which uses only the manuscript's own published values,
continues to reproduce them almost exactly).

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Reading key.} ``Verification: Yes'' means the structure was confirmed to match the manuscript's explicit description (a cited space group, or a recomputed bond distance compared directly). ``Partial'' means the geometry/chemistry is consistent with what the manuscript describes but no independent crystallographic citation allowed a strict confirmation. ``No'' means only the composition was verified: the manuscript does not specify a space group for that species, so the most reasonable MP/COD entry was used with no guarantee it is \emph{the} exact structure the authors used. This does not invalidate the \S\ref{sec:reframing} validation (which only compares the arithmetic reconstruction of the manuscript's own published ICOHP values, independent of any local structure) but it does bound the scope of any future comparison between our own DFT+LOBSTER calculations and the manuscript's numbers for these species.
