\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textsuperscript{a}~excludes a real but manuscript-unaccounted third-shell Sn-Sn contact ($d=3.54$--$3.57$~\AA, $-1.336$~eV/formula unit): the manuscript's own accounting for ZnSn is 6 Zn-Sn bonds $+$ 1 Zn-Zn bond per formula unit, with no Sn-Sn term at all, even though DFT+LOBSTER reports a genuine, well-separated first-shell Sn-Sn contact at that distance. A scope-of-accounting difference, not a shell-detection error.

\noindent\textsuperscript{b}~true first shell only (the 6 basal Zn-Zn bonds at $d=2.608$~\AA); see below for why the dataset's general first-shell detector instead sums 12 bonds here.

Both Sn and ZnSn were already resolved in the first pass of this
2026-08-18 update: replacing the default $\beta$-tin reference with the
manuscript's $\alpha$-tin phase, computed under its exact methodology,
drops Sn's gap from 38.4\% to 0.4\% --- almost the entire Sn gap was a
polymorph error, not a genuine DFT disagreement --- and excluding the
manuscript-unaccounted Sn-Sn term (note a) drops ZnSn from 8.1\% to
0.2\%.

Zn's 36.0\% residual, left open at the time, is now resolved as well.
Root cause (\texttt{analysis/compute\_manuscript\_validation\_icohp.py}):
\texttt{reaction\_analysis.nearest\_neighbor.detect\_first\_shell()} ---
the general first-shell detector this project uses dataset-wide via
\texttt{bond\_filter="nearest\_neighbor"} for its 588-compound bond-type
classification (\texttt{analysis/compute\_icohp\_icobi\_bondtype.py},
regression-tested in \texttt{tests/test\_nearest\_neighbor.py}) --- cuts
at the single LARGEST gap anywhere in a pair type's whole distance
spectrum, not the first one. Zn's anomalously low hcp $c/a$ ratio puts a
second, chemically distinct Zn-Zn shell (interlayer, $2.729$--$2.734$~\AA)
only $0.12$~\AA\ past the true first shell (basal, $2.608$~\AA) ---
much closer than the $\sim\!1.04$~\AA\ gap to the real third shell ---
so the detector locks onto that later gap and sums both shells (12
bonds instead of 6) as one, roughly doubling the ICOHP magnitude.
Restricting the sum to the true first shell only (note b) gives
$-12.649$~eV, $0.3\%$ from the manuscript's $-12.686$~eV.

This is a genuine limitation of applying one general-purpose
shell-detection rule across a dataset with widely varying coordination
geometries, not a DFT/LOBSTER methodology disagreement --- and it is
\emph{not} evidence against the general detector's own design choice
(the single-largest-gap rule is deliberate and correctly handles the
counter-example that motivated it, see \texttt{nearest\_neighbor.py}'s
docstring). \texttt{nearest\_neighbor.py} itself is left unchanged: Zn's
near-degenerate double shell is not necessarily representative of the
588-compound population, and whether any other compound's bond-type
classification is similarly affected has not been checked here --- that
would need its own dataset-wide audit before touching results elsewhere
in this project (bond\_type classification, the antibonding descriptor,
\ldots), and is flagged as a follow-up rather than attempted as part of
this table's fix.

With all three species corrected, the recomputed $\Delta$ICOHP for
ZnSn$\to$Zn+Sn ($-3.448$~eV, $-333$~kJ/mol) is now within $1.2\%$ of the
manuscript's $-3.489$~eV ($-337$~kJ/mol) --- down from $1.9\times$ before
this correction, and from $2.9\times$ in the original (pre-manuscript-
methodology) calculation. This project's own DFT+LOBSTER ICOHP
methodology reproduces the manuscript's reported values to within
$1.2\%$ once (i) the correct polymorphs are used, (ii) the manuscript's
own bond-accounting scope is matched, and (iii) first-coordination-shell
summation is not confounded by near-degenerate shells --- closing the
validation question raised in \S\ref{sec:reframing} and confirming this
project's ICOHP methodology, not just its arithmetic, matches the
manuscript's (\S\ref{sec:reframing}'s own 7-case validation, using only
the manuscript's published values, was never in question and continues
to reproduce them almost exactly).

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Reading key.} ``Verification: Yes'' means the structure was confirmed to match the manuscript's explicit description (a cited space group, or a recomputed bond distance compared directly). ``Partial'' means the geometry/chemistry is consistent with what the manuscript describes but no independent crystallographic citation allowed a strict confirmation. ``No'' means only the composition was verified: the manuscript does not specify a space group for that species, so the most reasonable MP/COD entry was used with no guarantee it is \emph{the} exact structure the authors used. This does not invalidate the \S\ref{sec:reframing} validation (which only compares the arithmetic reconstruction of the manuscript's own published ICOHP values, independent of any local structure) but it does bound the scope of any future comparison between our own DFT+LOBSTER calculations and the manuscript's numbers for these species.
