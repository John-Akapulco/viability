\section{Dataset Composition and Selection Criteria}
\label{sec:si-dataset}

Candidate structures were drawn from the Materials Project database by
querying unary and binary chemical systems, restricted to non-magnetic
structures (absolute total magnetization $\leq 0.01~\mu_\text{B}$ per
formula unit, computed directly from the stored magnetic moments) and
excluding $f$-block elements. Three complementary selection tracks were
combined into the final 597-structure dataset:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Track 1} (186 compounds): $\leq 20$ atoms/cell, balanced
  across stability classes (60 per family), with a cap of two entries per
  chemical system to maximize elemental diversity over redundant polymorphs.
  \item \textbf{Track 2} (163 compounds/elements): $\leq 40$ atoms/cell,
  targeting additional elemental references, off-equilibrium polymorphs
  (high-pressure phases, allotropes), and alkali/alkaline-earth binaries
  against N, O, F, P, S, and Cl, chosen to build genuine same-composition
  polymorph groups and probe descriptor behavior far from equilibrium.
  \item \textbf{Track 3} (45 binary compounds): an adversarial robustness
  set pairing an experimentally known compound sitting farthest above the
  convex hull of its chemical system with one to three theoretical
  polymorphs of the same formula lying even farther above the hull.
\end{itemize}

Table~\ref{tab:si-composition} summarizes the dataset by element count and
Table~\ref{tab:si-stability} by stability class. One trivial-cell reference
element yielded no usable LOBSTER step and is excluded from every
ICOHP/ICOBI-derived statistic (Table~\ref{tab:si-composition}, footnote).

classification for all 588 entries, listing formula, Materials Project

ICOHP/ICOBI antibonding population, is reproduced by bonding-type category

\section{LOBSTER Quality Control}
\label{sec:si-qc}

Every LOBSTER run was screened using the fraction of $k$-points failing
projection orthonormalization (\texttt{lobsterout}) and the maximum
deviation reported in \texttt{bandOverlaps.lobster}, with a red-flag
threshold of 0.1 on the latter (main text, Section~2.7). A systematic sweep
of the full 597-structure dataset under the conservative combined criterion
(100\% $k$-point failure \emph{and} band-overlap deviation $>0.5$
\emph{and} robust $z$-score $>10$ on the resulting $\Delta$(ICOHP)) flagged
a single compound, a copper--gold intermetallic (Cu$_4$Au), for exclusion:

\begin{itemize}
  \item 343/343 $k$-points fail orthonormalization (100\%, not a borderline
  case);
  \item maximum \texttt{bandOverlaps.lobster} deviation of 11.4 (versus the
  0.1 red-flag threshold);
  \item physically implausible ICOHP values (third-neighbor Cu--Cu contacts
  at 4.25--5.04~\AA{} with ICOHP values of $+7.6$ to $+11.6$~eV, an order
  of magnitude too large to be physical);
  \item a $\Delta$(ICOHP)/atom of $-380.5$~eV, $\sim$27$\times$ the
  next-worst case in the rest of the population.
\end{itemize}

The second-worst case identified by the sweep is already 29$\times$ less
extreme than Cu$_4$Au and is plausibly genuine, dense $d$-electron-rich
intermetallic chemistry rather than a projection failure, confirming
Cu$_4$Au as a unique quality-control case rather than a symptom of a wider
problem. Every statistic reported in the main text has been computed on the
remaining 588 (respectively 438--439 case-1-reaction) structures; the
excluded compound's own VASP+LOBSTER output is retained in the data
repository for reference.

\section{Compute Cost}
\label{sec:si-cost}

Table~\ref{tab:si-cost} summarizes VASP and LOBSTER wall-clock time across
the 597 structures. Every job ran on a single SLURM node allocation (16
cores): VASP under MPI parallelism, then LOBSTER under OpenMP parallelism on
the same 16 cores, sequentially within the same job.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{VASP and LOBSTER compute time (597 structures).}
\label{tab:si-cost}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
\toprule
 & VASP & LOBSTER \\
\midrule
$n$ & 597 & 596\textsuperscript{a} \\
Mean (s) & 188 & 578 \\
Median (s) & 72 & 157 \\
Max (s) & 5715 (1.6 h) & 22555 (6.3 h) \\
Sequential total & 31.2 h & 95.8 h \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\\{\footnotesize \textsuperscript{a} one of 597 structures had no usable
LOBSTER step (trivial-cell reference element).}
\end{table}

Combined sequential total (VASP + LOBSTER, 597 structures): 127.0~h ---
between two and three orders of magnitude below what a phonon
(supercell-based) or AIMD screening of the same 597 structures, at the
hierarchy described in the main text's Introduction, would require, and
consistent with the descriptor's intended role as an inexpensive
pre-screen rather than a replacement for that hierarchy.
